About

The name is a
line of women
who made things.

My mother is Scilla, and Scillasdotter is her name, given to her daughters. So the name on the door is not a label I chose. It is where I come from.

I trained in women's tailoring and hold a journeyman's certificate, so I know this craft from the pattern up. In 2009 I opened my own atelier and spent five years doing every part of it myself.

Then I went to learn the other half. Ten years of buying, planning, margins, teams and process, next to the people who build collections for a living. I know how a collection is costed, how a floor is run, and how a season actually gets made.

And I spent years on the road. Wardrobe tailor on tour with Lady Gaga, Pink, Rihanna and Cirque du Soleil, and designer and stylist at Eurovision for three years. A stage teaches you things a fitting room cannot. Nothing gets a second take.

Those are production credits, so I can name them. What I make for a private client stays between us, and it always will.

Artist collections built from nothing. Couture. Tailored suits. Wedding dresses. A whole wardrobe for a tour. The work changes shape every time. The method does not.

Everything is made in house, from the first sketch to the last stitch, so nothing leaves here that I have not seen at every stage. Sheer cloth against a structured line, a delicate detail inside a bold shape. I pick fabric for how long it will last, not for how it photographs this season. A piece should still be worth wearing in ten years.

Outside the atelier I compete in bikini fitness. It is the same discipline as this work. You measure, you adjust, you do it again, and you find out what you are actually capable of. I have never been interested in the average version of anything.

I am in Amsterdam, and I am taking commissions again.

Alexandra Scillasdotter

Where it was learned

  1. 2006 Max Mara, Stockholm. Where I found out that a garment has a price long before it has a story, and that the two are not enemies.
  2. 2009 My own atelier. Couture and made to measure, my hands on every stage.
  3. 2010 On the road. Wardrobe tailor on Lady Gaga, The Monster Ball, and on Pink, The Funhouse Carnival Tour. Stage clothes are built to a different standard: quick changes, hard light, and the same piece worn night after night.
  4. 2011 Wardrobe tailor on Rihanna, Last Girl on Earth, and on Cirque du Soleil, Michael Jackson The Immortal World Tour. Two tours, one year, and no room anywhere for a seam that gives.
  5. 2012 Eurovision. Designer and stylist, three years running. Live television, one take, no second chance.
  6. 2014 Massimo Dutti. A floor, a team and a season to keep moving. I learned how a house breathes, and how to teach someone else to cut.
  7. 2019 Acne Studios, then Moncler. Years next to people who build collections for a living, and years of seeing what it costs to keep that up.
  8. 2026 Amsterdam. Back at the table, with all of it.

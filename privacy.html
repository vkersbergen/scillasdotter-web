Privacy

What happens to what you send me

Short version: what you write in the form comes to my inbox and stays there. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared, and this site does not follow you around.

The form

The form asks for your name and a way to reach you, and for whatever you want to tell me. It is delivered to couture@scillasdotter.com by FormSubmit, a service that does nothing but forward the message. I keep enquiries so I can answer them and pick the conversation up later. Ask me to delete yours and it is gone the same day.

This website

No cookies. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no tracking of any kind. The pages are plain files served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses for security. The typefaces are served from this domain, so opening a page here does not announce your visit to anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to couture@scillasdotter.com and you get an answer from me, not from a system.
